What this is not

We are not a hospital, insurer, medical provider, or billing advocate, and nothing here is medical advice. We surface published price data so you can ask better questions and compare options before scheduling.

Published prices can differ from your final bill. Hospital price files list cash/self-pay and standard charge rates — your actual insurance-negotiated rate may be different, and additional charges (facility fees, anesthesia, a separate radiologist reading fee) can apply depending on how the procedure is billed.

Always confirm directly with the facility and your insurer before scheduling, and use this to compare options and ask informed questions — not as a guarantee of your final cost.
