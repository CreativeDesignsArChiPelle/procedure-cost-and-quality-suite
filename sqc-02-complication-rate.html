What this is not

We are not a hospital, surgeon, insurer, or medical provider, and nothing here is medical advice. We surface public outcomes data so you can ask better questions and compare options.

A track record is not a guarantee. Public outcomes data reflects past results across a hospital’s or surgeon’s patients generally, adjusted at a population level — it does not predict any individual outcome, and small sample sizes for rare procedures can make single-year figures noisy.

Always discuss directly with your doctor and the facility, and use this to compare options and ask informed questions — not as a substitute for medical judgment.
